Observed failures and comparison eligibility

180 process trials issued 114750 measured requests, plus 1800 warmup requests.
The collection completed its schedule. Its status remains failed: completing the
schedule does not turn failed delivery or failed requests into successful tests.

BAML medium, http-hello-500, trial 0
  trials/0033-baml-medium/ served all 1500 measured requests correctly. The BTEL
  worker and processor panicked with TransportFailed; telemetry_ok was false.
  The recording contains 1443 handler calls instead of 1510, and 1443 successful
  roots instead of 1511. Expected counts include ten warmup requests and main.
  This trial is excluded from every HTTP performance bar. It cannot establish
  the cost of successfully delivered, complete telemetry.
  stderr.txt and telemetry.json retain the failure. The panic occurs in the
  worker-binding error path; these logs do not establish which SetupError caused it.

Bun node:http, http-hello-500, trial 3
  trials/0081-bun-not_applicable/ contains five request timeouts out of 1500.
  Client dispatch p99 remained below the 10 ms limit. The remaining 1495 responses
  passed validation. This is retained as a request outcome, including its full
  deadline-extended throughput denominator. Successful-response percentiles do
  not include timed-out requests; the failure counts must be read beside them.
  Ten additional diagnostic processes, each with 1500 measured requests and ten
  warmups, did not reproduce the timeout. Their server connection, handler and
  finish counters each reached 1510. Added instrumentation changes behavior;
  this does not disprove the original observation or identify its cause.
  Diagnostic sources and observations: ../http-timeout-diagnostic/.

Driver scheduling exclusions
